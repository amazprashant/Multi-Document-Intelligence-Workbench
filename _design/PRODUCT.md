# Product – Multi-Document Intelligence Workbench

**Users:** compliance analysts, underwriters and reviewers who must reconcile several documents about one entity.
**Job to be done:** "Tell me where these documents disagree or are incomplete, and prove it."
**Principles:** traceable over clever · facts and judgement visibly separate · fail per file, not per batch · no data leaves unless a key is configured.
**Success:** reviewer spots all planted discrepancies in the sample set in under a minute and can open each quote.
**Non-goals:** OCR, legal advice, editing documents, multi-user collaboration.
